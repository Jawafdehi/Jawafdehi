The two WOFF2 files in public/font/Geist/ are the LATIN subsets of the variable
fonts, taken from the Google Fonts CDN (Geist v5, Geist Mono v6) rather than
built by scripts/fonts/build-webfonts.sh — the same route Inter took, and the
reason there is no TTF for them in this directory. Both carry a single `wght`
axis.

They exist for ONE page, /likhit, which is deliberately typeset in the likhit
project's own typefaces rather than the site's Inter/Vesper Libre pair. They are
declared in src/pages/likhit.css, not src/index.css, so no other route pays for
them.
